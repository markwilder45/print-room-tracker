Staffing assumptions

Productive hours leave out breaks and meetings. Forecast staffing divides labor hours by this number.

Products and steps

Drag the dotted handle (or use ↑ ↓) to set the order steps appear in the floor log. Distributors are logged on the top step only. Printer steps don't appear on the floor log; their target is the print rate from the printers' job logs. Target = units per hour (your sheet's Hourly Target). Share = fraction of units that go through the step. Overhead steps use hours per day.
